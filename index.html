About

I'm a Senior Applied Scientist at Amazon, on the Perfect Order Experience science team. I work on large language models — continued pretraining, instruction tuning, and the evaluation and training infrastructure that makes them usable in production.

Most of my recent work has gone into a domain-specific foundation model for seller-facing applications: designing the training pipelines, building data recipes for pretraining and fine-tuning mixtures, and handling the multilingual and deployment problems that show up when a model has to serve real traffic.

Alongside that, I collaborate on academic research in robustness and evaluation — certified robustness at scale, adversarial attacks on vision-language models, and benchmarks for LLM-based judges. Before Amazon I was a deep learning engineer at the Bosch Center for Artificial Intelligence. I hold an M.S. from Carnegie Mellon and a B.S. from Ohio State.

Happy to talk about LLM training, evaluation, or applied ML — get in touch.

Projects

Seller Foundation Model Amazon · 2024 – present

A domain-specific foundation model (8B–14B) that unifies seller-facing applications behind one backbone. I led the end-to-end design of the training pipelines — continued pretraining, instruction fine-tuning, and evaluation — across heterogeneous seller data.

Much of the gain came from data work rather than modeling: building recipes over domain-specific mixtures, sampling strategies, and weighting schemes. Shipping it meant solving multilingual support, data heterogeneity, and production deployment at scale.

Product Quality Detection Amazon · 2022 – 2023

NLP models that analyze customer feedback to surface product quality issues. I owned the full lifecycle — data curation, model research, training, evaluation, and containerized deployment — and worked with the business team to establish procedures for maintenance and retraining.

The final model improved key metrics 10% over the existing system, with an estimated downstream impact of $12B.

Image Segmentation Bosch Center for AI · 2020 – 2021

Performance analysis and modeling for an image segmentation system, improving accuracy 4.5%. Worked across the full lifecycle — scoping, data processing, modeling, and deployment to both cloud and local environments — and prototyped solutions with multi-disciplinary teams to assess feasibility, cost, and scalability.
